How the market moves: every bet joins the YES or NO pool. Price of YES = YES pool ÷ total pool (with 10 virtual coins seeded per side so the first bet doesn't slam the needle).
Payouts (parimutuel): if your side wins you split the whole real pool in proportion to your stake — payout = stake × total ÷ winning pool. Losers pay winners; no house cut.
